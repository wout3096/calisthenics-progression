Quick start

1. Browse the Library and open an exercise.
2. Choose a suitable variation, without rushing levels.
3. Record your practice in Log and review it in Progress.

This is a general reference, not a personalised coaching plan. Progressions are suggestions, not a test or a ladder you must complete. Use controlled technique, allow recovery, warm up, and stop if you feel pain. Ask a qualified coach to check unfamiliar or advanced skills.
